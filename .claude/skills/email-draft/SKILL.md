# Email Draft

Drafts professional, direct CRE emails. Reads context to write in Akiva's voice.

---

## Process

1. Read `context/me.md` to understand Akiva's voice.
2. Read `context/portfolio.md` if the email references a property or tenant.
3. Ask for any missing context (if not provided in the request).
4. Draft the email.
5. Offer a subject line if not provided.

---

## Required Inputs

- Recipient (name + relationship -- tenant, broker, prospect, vendor, attorney, etc.)
- Purpose (LOI response, lease question, tour request, follow-up, vacancy notice, etc.)
- Key points to hit (can be bullet notes or a voice transcript)
- Tone preference (optional -- defaults to professional and direct)

---

## Email Structure

**Subject:** Specific and concrete. Not "Following Up" -- instead "LOI response -- 2,400 SF at 1364 Briar Vista" or "Tour confirmed Thursday 2pm."

**Opening:** Use their name. One sentence that's contextual -- reference the last interaction, the property in question, or a specific detail.

**Body:** 2-4 short paragraphs or bullets. One idea per paragraph. Always include the property address, SF, and rate when relevant. No walls of text.

**Close:** Clear next step. Sign off with Akiva's name.

---

## Tone

- Direct, professional, operator voice -- not stiff, not casual
- Include specifics -- address, SF, rate per SF, lease term, dates
- Short sentences -- brokers and tenants are reading on their phones
- No filler phrases: "I hope this email finds you well," "Please don't hesitate to reach out," "As per my last email"
- Never use exclamation points more than once per email
- "AI should be invisible" -- never sound like AI wrote it
