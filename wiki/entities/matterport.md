# Matterport

AI-powered 3D virtual tour platform. Central to Akiva's leasing process.

---

## What It Is

Capture 3D photos of a space, Matterport stitches them into a navigable virtual tour with AI-driven dollhouse, floor plan, and measurement tools.

---

## How Akiva Uses It

- Every available space gets a Matterport tour
- Matterport URL goes in every flyer + listing email
- Brokers and prospects can self-tour before showing up in person
- Cuts down on wasted in-person tours, lets out-of-market prospects evaluate quickly

---

## Adjacent Workflow

- Photos pulled from Matterport tours → [[Gemini]] for AI staging / destaging → final marketing photos for flyers and listings
