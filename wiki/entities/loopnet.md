# LoopNet

"Zillow for commercial." Where Akiva gets inbound leads on his commercial listings.

---

## What It Is

Commercial real estate listing marketplace. Listings get views, prospects fill out a contact form, and Akiva receives the inquiry as an email.

---

## How It Fits Into Akiva's Stack

**Lead flow:** Listing inquiry → email to Akiva → currently lives in his inbox → goal: parse and log into [[AppFolio]] + [[portfolio.md]] automatically.

---

## Pain Points

- Inquiries are unstructured email, not API webhooks
- No automatic logging into the CRM ([[AppFolio]])
- Some inquiries are quality leads, others are tire-kickers -- no scoring
- He has to manually copy contact info into followup tools
