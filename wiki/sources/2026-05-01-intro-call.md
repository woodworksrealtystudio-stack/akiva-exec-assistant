# 2026-05-01 Intro Call -- Akiva + Eli

First call between [[Eli Bock]] and [[Akiva Halpern]]. Captured the three pain points this assistant is built around.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Akiva** is a one-man CRE operator in Toco Hills, recently licensed to broker third-party deals on top of his own owned portfolio
- He's a heavy daily ChatGPT user, no agentic experience -- "I 100% lean into AI"
- He came prepared with a 3-column doc: Akiva (what he does), VA (what he'd outsource), Leasing Agent (what a future hire would do). His goal: "I want those things in all three of those columns that aren't me, I wanna be leaning on AI, or automation as much as possible."
- The Context OS framing landed: *"that is ultimately what I would like to be able to have control of, maybe tweaking it, managing it"*
- He explicitly described wanting to be the "AI manager" before he hires a human VA

## Three Pain Points (in his order)

1. **[[Lead Consolidation]]** -- "everything to end up in my CRM"
2. **[[Vacancy Marketing]]** -- "create a new flyer... send that flyer to my email list of tenants, brokers, retailers"
3. **[[Email Triage]]** + Light EA -- calendar, inbox, drafted replies

## Tools He Already Uses

- [[AppFolio]] (property mgmt, target CRM)
- [[LoopNet]] (lead source, email-based)
- [[Google Voice]] (inbound calls + texts)
- [[Matterport]] (3D virtual tours -- central to his leasing flow)
- Gemini (photo staging / destaging)
- ChatGPT (daily driver)

## What He Doesn't Want

- Bookkeeping ("rather a person doing bookkeeping")
- Anything locked behind a vendor UI he can't tweak
- Mass automation that strips the personal touch
