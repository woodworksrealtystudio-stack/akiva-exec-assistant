## How I Work

I run a one-man operation. I manage and lease commercial properties I own, and I just got licensed to start brokering deals for third parties as well. I use AI all day -- ChatGPT for drafting, emails, lease language, even personal growth -- but I haven't gotten into agentic systems yet. I want to.

I'm not non-technical. I just need things to be open and tweakable, not locked behind someone else's UI. The point of this assistant is that I own it, I run it, and I can extend it as I figure out what I want it to do.

The frame I want is "AI manager" -- I run the system, I tweak it, and once it's mature, that's the basis for hiring an actual VA or leasing agent who can plug in on top of it.

## My Philosophy on AI

- AI should be invisible to the people I work with -- tenants, brokers, prospects shouldn't know AI helped draft an email
- I want to be the one running it, not paying someone else to run it for me
- ChatGPT is great at chat; agentic systems are the next layer -- they reach into my CRM, my Gmail, my Calendar, my data
- Bookkeeping I'd rather have a human do; everything else is fair game

---

## What I Don't Want

- Generic AI emails that sound like AI
- Locked-down vendor UIs where I can't see what's running underneath
- Mass automation that removes the personal touch with my tenants and brokers
- Anything that requires me to learn a new dashboard every week

## Relationship-First Operator Style

Important context for any tenant-facing communication:

- I have ~50 tenants. It's been very much relationship-based.
- I've never really charged late fees. When tenants are struggling, I work with them, not against them.
- I'd rather lose a month's late fee than damage a long-term tenant relationship.
- Don't draft aggressive collection language, escalation threats, or formal late notices unless I explicitly ask. Default to soft, human, "let's figure this out" tone.
- This applies to maintenance requests too -- treat tenants like long-term partners, not transactions.

If something feels legally adversarial (eviction, dispute, attorney-mentioned), flag it for me to handle personally. Don't auto-draft.
